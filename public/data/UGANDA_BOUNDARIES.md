Uganda district polygons are from the Uganda Bureau of Statistics 2020 administrative boundary layer, distributed through the UNOSAT GeoData REST service.

Source: https://unosat-geodrr.cern.ch/data/rest/services/Hosted/UGA_District_Boundary/FeatureServer/23

The polygon layer contains 135 district boundaries. The application's separate Electoral Commission 2022 hierarchy contains 145 district and city records; independently administered cities therefore remain available in the hierarchy list even where the boundary source does not provide a separate city polygon.
